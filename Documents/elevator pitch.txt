"gnome game" is a top-down dungeon crawler starring a gnome in a whimsical fantasy world. Use magic to fight through dungeons, gaining experience and resources, and rescuing trapped allies on the way to securing a precious dragon egg

Main focuses toward development
UI(hud, stat bars, in-level progress, menus, popups)
Wizard(dialogue, quests, story prog)
Player(movement, camera, casting spells)
Magic(spell structure, creation, progression)
Overworld(navigation, progression, enter/exit maps)
Dungeons(structure, layout, objects)
Enemies(basic, bosses)
